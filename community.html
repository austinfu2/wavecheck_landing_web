Community Rules

Effective November 3, 2026

Reports from the beach only work if surfers can trust them. These rules cover everything you post in WaveCheck: ratings, notes and photos. You agree to them before your first report.

What a good report looks like

  • You're at the spot, saying what you can see right now.
  • It's honest, and rated for the board you picked.
  • Any photo shows the waves, not people.

What's not allowed

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. That includes:

  • Abuse, harassment, threats or hate towards anyone
  • Sexual, violent or otherwise objectionable content
  • Photos that single out other people, and any photo of a child
  • Other people's personal information
  • Spam, advertising, links or promoting a business
  • False reports, including reports made just to earn free Pro

Flagging and blocking

Open the reports for a spot and tap the menu on any report. Choose Report this to flag it, or Block this surfer to stop seeing their reports. A report you flag disappears for you straight away, and you can undo a block in Settings, under Blocked surfers.

We review every flag within 24 hours. You can also reach us at hello@wavecheck.surf.

If the rules are broken

We remove reports that break these rules. For repeat or serious offences we close the account, and any free Pro earned with false reports can be withdrawn.

Free Pro for reporting

Report on 5 days and you get a month of Pro, free. Report on 8 days in a calendar month and you get another month. One report a day counts, and it has to be made at the spot. We may change or end this offer, but free months you've already earned stay yours unless they were earned by breaking these rules.

Your reports

Delete any of your reports from the menu on it. Deleting your account deletes all your reports and photos. Our Privacy Policy explains what we store.
